After deployment, actual repurchase outcomes are tracked and compared against predictions. Model health is monitored on an ongoing basis using AUC/GINI, Lift/Gain, calibration, and the observed repurchase rate broken down by score band.

In a real marketing campaign, a treatment/control group design is required to measure the \emph{incremental} impact of targeting -- that is, the effect of the campaign beyond what would have happened anyway. Because the Olist dataset does not contain marketing treatment or response data, revenue, cost, and profit/ROI figures for this project are presented as a \textbf{business simulation} based on stated assumptions, rather than as a measured causal impact.

Data is analyzed at the transaction, invoice, customer, and product levels in order to understand the population before any feature engineering or label generation takes place:

\begin{itemize}
    \item Number of transactions, invoices, customers, and products in the dataset.
    \item Missing \textbf{Customer ID} values.
    \item Cancellations and other invalid records.
    \item Revenue over time.
    \item Proportion of one-time versus repeat customers.
    \item Purchase frequency and inter-purchase intervals.
    \item RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) segmentation of customer behavior.
\end{itemize}

The distribution of inter-purchase intervals is analyzed explicitly to determine an appropriate churn definition and prediction horizon, and to confirm that there is enough history before each snapshot date and enough future data after it to observe whether a customer remains active.

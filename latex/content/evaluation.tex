\section{Technical Metrics}

Model discrimination and classification quality are measured using standard technical metrics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item ROC-AUC and GINI.
    \item Precision, Recall, and F1-score.
    \item PR-AUC (precision-recall area under the curve), which is particularly informative under class imbalance.
\end{itemize}

\section{Business / Ranking Metrics}

Since the model is ultimately used to rank and target customers, ranking-oriented metrics are reported alongside the technical metrics:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Lift@5\% / Lift@10\%} -- how much better the top-ranked segment performs relative to the overall base rate.
    \item \textbf{Gain@10\%} -- the proportion of all repurchasers captured within the top 10\% of ranked customers.
    \item \textbf{Precision@10\%} -- the repurchase rate observed within the top 10\% of ranked customers.
\end{itemize}

Calibration of the predicted probabilities is also assessed where relevant. The overriding evaluation criterion is the model's ability to rank customers well within the top $X\%$ that a marketing team can realistically target, rather than raw classification accuracy alone.

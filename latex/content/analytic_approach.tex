The problem is framed as a \textbf{binary classification} task: predict the probability that a customer will make another purchase within the next 30 days.

\paragraph{Population.} The population consists of customers who have purchased at least once. First-time buyers are evaluated with respect to their second purchase, while repeat buyers are evaluated with respect to their next subsequent purchase.

\paragraph{Use of the model output.} The predicted probability is used as a ranking signal, denoted $P(\text{repurchase})$. Customers can equivalently be ranked by a churn-risk score,
\begin{equation}
    \text{churn risk} = 1 - P(\text{repurchase}),
\end{equation}
and the resulting ranking is used to select the top $X\%$ of customers for marketing targeting.

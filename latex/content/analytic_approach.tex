The problem is framed as a \textbf{binary classification} task: predict the probability that an existing customer will become inactive within a defined future period.

\paragraph{Population.} The population consists of customers with identifiable purchase history, i.e.\ customers who can be traced across transactions using a customer identifier.

\paragraph{Use of the model output.} The predicted probability is used as a ranking signal, denoted $P(\text{churn})$, equivalent to the probability that a customer makes no purchase during the future period:
\begin{equation}
    P(\text{churn}) = P(\text{no purchase within the future period}).
\end{equation}
Customers are ranked by this churn-risk score, and the resulting ranking is used to select the top $X\%$ of customers for retention marketing.

This project addresses \textbf{customer repurchase prediction} for e-commerce marketing optimization. The goal is to build a system that predicts whether an existing customer is likely to make another purchase within the next 30 days. This prediction supports two concrete business needs: improving customer retention, and making marketing spend more efficient by prioritizing the customers who are most likely to respond to a retention or reactivation campaign.

The system is developed and evaluated on the \textbf{Olist Brazilian E-commerce dataset}, which provides order-, customer-, product-, payment-, and review-level records from a real online marketplace. Working with this dataset grounds the modeling approach in realistic purchasing behavior, while also imposing realistic constraints -- most notably, the dataset contains no direct record of marketing treatment or response, which limits how causal claims about campaign impact can be made later in the project.

This project addresses \textbf{customer churn prediction} for e-commerce retention marketing. The goal is to build a system that predicts whether an existing customer is likely to become inactive within a defined future period. This prediction supports two concrete business needs: improving customer retention, and making marketing spend more efficient by prioritizing the customers who are most at risk of churning for a retention campaign.

The system is developed and evaluated on the \textbf{UCI Online Retail II dataset}, which provides transaction-level records -- invoice, product, quantity, price, timestamp, customer, and country -- from a UK-based online retailer. Working with this dataset grounds the modeling approach in realistic purchasing behavior, while also imposing realistic constraints -- most notably, the dataset contains no direct record of marketing treatment or response, which limits how causal claims about campaign impact can be made later in the project.

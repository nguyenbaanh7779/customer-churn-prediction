This report summarizes the model's predictive performance and its ability to rank customers by repurchase likelihood. The Random/No-model, RFM rule-based, and ML targeting strategies are compared at different top $X\%$ targeting levels, showing the incremental value each approach adds over the previous one.

Estimated revenue, cost, and profit/ROI are reported under explicitly defined campaign assumptions. A key limitation of this analysis is that the Olist dataset does not contain marketing treatment/control data, so these financial figures should be interpreted as a business simulation rather than a validated measurement of campaign impact. Confirming the real-world impact of ML-based targeting would require running an actual campaign with a treatment/control design.

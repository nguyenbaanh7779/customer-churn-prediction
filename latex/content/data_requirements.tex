The primary data source is the \textbf{Olist Brazilian E-commerce Dataset}. The following tables are used to build customer purchase history and behavioral features:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Customers} -- customer identifiers and location information.
    \item \textbf{Orders} -- order-level records, including order status and timestamps.
    \item \textbf{Order Items} -- line-item detail for each order, including product, price, and freight cost.
    \item \textbf{Products} -- product category and attributes.
    \item \textbf{Payments} -- payment type, installments, and value.
    \item \textbf{Reviews} -- customer review scores associated with orders.
\end{itemize}

These tables are joined to construct an analytical view of customer behavior over time, at the transaction, order, customer, and product levels.

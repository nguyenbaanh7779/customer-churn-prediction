\section{Data Requirement Overview}
\label{sec:4-1}

Requirements derive from the business and analytical objectives: identify customers, purchases, products, revenue, customer history, temporal behavior and future outcomes.

\section{Customer Information}
\label{sec:4-2}

Customer ID and Country. Customer ID is required to build customer histories and snapshots.

\section{Transaction Information}
\label{sec:4-3}

Invoice, InvoiceDate, Customer ID, StockCode, Description, Quantity and Price. Use the actual field names of the selected dataset (Online Retail II uses \texttt{Invoice}, \texttt{Price} and \texttt{Customer ID}).

\section{Revenue Information}
\label{sec:4-4}

Transaction revenue is derived as Quantity $\times$ Price and can be aggregated by customer, purchase occasion and month.

\section{Product Information}
\label{sec:4-5}

StockCode and Description support product-level and customer-product analysis. Product cost, category hierarchy and margin are not available.

\section{Temporal Information}
\label{sec:4-6}

InvoiceDate is required for observation windows, prediction windows, monthly metrics, inter-purchase intervals, lifecycle analysis and temporal splits.

\section{Marketing and Cost Information}
\label{sec:4-7}

A production system would ideally collect CampaignID, CustomerID, CampaignDate, CampaignType, Channel, Treatment, Discount, VoucherCost, MarketingCost and campaign outcomes. None of these are in the current dataset; the related quantities in Chapter~\ref{ch:evaluation} are assumptions.

\section{Analytical Dataset Design}
\label{sec:4-8}

Each final modeling row represents \textbf{Customer $\times$ Snapshot Date}, with historical features and a future churn label.
